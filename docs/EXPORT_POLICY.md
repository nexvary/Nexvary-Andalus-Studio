# Export & Quantity Policy

Stage 625 introduces deterministic DXF and BOM CSV outputs.

- Geometry exports derive from the deterministic project model, never from visual AI pixels.
- DXF currently exports 2D wall centerlines in R12-compatible ASCII form.
- BOM quantities are estimates derived from assigned surface area, material coverage and waste.
- No default monetary price is embedded. Cost appears only when a user supplies a unit price and currency.
- Every future downloadable export should be accompanied by an Export Manifest tied to the project fingerprint.
- These outputs are design/estimating aids and require professional verification before fabrication or construction.
